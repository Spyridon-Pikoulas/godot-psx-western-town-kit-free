# PSX Western Town Kit Free

**15 free modular PS1-style pieces to build a general store's front** for your western game: a plank floor, white clapboard walls with a doorway, a shopfront and a corner post, the store's door and its window lettered DRY GOODS, false fronts square and peaked, the GENERAL STORE board, a boardwalk under a tin awning on its porch posts, a hitching post and a tumbleweed rolling by. They snap to a 2 m grid. Low-poly and point-filtered, a scene per piece with its colliders, ready to drop in. Free for commercial use.

### What's inside

- **Structure:** a plank floor, a white clapboard wall, its doorway, a shopfront and a corner post,
  on a 2 m grid, 3 m high; false fronts square and peaked over them.
- **The store:** its half-glazed door, swinging open with its collider, the DRY GOODS window and
  the GENERAL STORE board.
- **The boardwalk:** a boardwalk cell, a tin awning and its porch posts, a hitching post.
- **A tumbleweed** rolling to and fro.
- **2 animations** on the moving parts, as clips in GLB and takes in FBX.
- **15 icons**, 256x256 PNGs with transparency, and the seamless floor and wall textures as their
  own PNGs.
- One texture atlas, shading baked into the vertex colours, real-world scale.

### Godot 4.3 to 4.7

An addon with a scene per piece: box colliders on every part (the doorway's leave the way through
clear), an AnimationPlayer where it moves, one lit material so your lamps fall on it, and a
gallery. Tested on Godot 4.3 and 4.7, on Forward+, Mobile and Compatibility.

```gdscript
# door: the piece in your level
door.get_node("AnimationPlayer").play("open")
```

For the full PS1 look, [PSX Look](https://heyheythere.itch.io/psx-look) turns the kit (and your
level) over in one line: wobbling vertices, warping textures, 240p, dither and 15-bit colour. Its
[free sampler](https://heyheythere.itch.io/psx-look-free) has the vertex snap and affine textures.
